Reporting a bug

When something is not working, please include:

  • Your device and OS (for example, iPhone 15 / iOS 18, or Pixel 8 / Android 15)
  • What happened, and what you expected instead
  • Steps to reproduce, if you can

That detail helps us find and fix the issue faster.
