Common questions

Can I start on a day other than January 1?

Yes — Day 1 begins whenever your family does. During onboarding you choose Today, Tomorrow, Next Sunday, or Next Monday. You can reset this any time in Settings.

I lost my progress / switched phones. Can I recover it?

All data is stored locally on your device, not on our servers, so it cannot be transferred automatically. If you back up your phone to iCloud or Google, restoring from that backup will restore the app data as well.

The Bible text isn't loading. What should I do?

Scripture is downloaded from bible-api.com and cached on your device. If you're offline and a chapter hasn't been cached yet, it won't load until you have a connection. Use the "Download upcoming week" option on the Reading screen while connected to pre-load content.

How do I turn off the daily reminder?

Go to Settings → Daily reminder and select "No reminder."

How do I reset the app and start over?

Go to Settings → scroll to the bottom → "Reset all progress." This clears your reading history, streaks, journal, and prayer list. It cannot be undone.

Is my family's data private?

Yes — see our Privacy Policy. We collect no data. Everything stays on your device.

Report a bug

If something isn't working, email contact@forgefaithmedia.com with a description of what happened and which screen you were on. A screenshot helps if you can attach one.
